The Mural

The Ancestors are Watching

Created by Atlanta-based artist ARRRTaddict (Lisette Correa), this mural is a tribute to the Taíno — the indigenous people of the Caribbean whose culture, language, and legacy continue to resonate across the Americas. The work draws on traditional Taíno iconography and cosmology.

The mural is one of a series of public artworks selected for Altera Media's ARtlanta initiative, chosen for both its visual power and its cultural significance during the 2026 FIFA World Cup in Atlanta.

The Inspiration + Content

Preserving History Digitally

Traditional American history curriculum focuses heavily on Western European narratives, while the foundational history of Native Americans such as the Taíno is often glossed over or treated as a footnote of 1492. The chosen mural is a response to this shortcoming and does a wonderful job at depicting Taíno cultural symbols, but the significance of this iconography was unknown to me without extra research. I realized I could use this very unfamiliarity as the driving force behind the AR experience, creating an informational layer on top of the mural to make this indigenous culture feel real, tactile, and close, while making their modern impact known to a new generation and global audience.

This project used Meshy and Blender to create 3D replications of real Taíno artifacts located in the Taíno Museum and the Metropolitan Museum of Art, bringing these distant relics to Atlanta and into viewer's own hands.

To deepen the immersion, the background music track was generated via Suno, aiming to replicate Taíno soundscapes through their monophonic musical structures and the acoustic textures of their traditional instruments like the mayohuacán, bone flutes, and seed rattles.

Through a 6-part interactive experience, we explore the pre-Columbus Taíno society, their ancestor-centric spiritual beliefs, the role of music in their culture, the mythology of the iconic Coquí tree frog, connect back to the World Cup through their community-gathering sports traditions, and shine a light on their modern legacy through descendants in Atlanta.

The AR Experience

Where the Past Meets the Present

This augmented-reality installation expands on the history of the Taíno people as depicted in the mural. Through their phones, viewers can explore the symbols, artifacts, and traditions of the Taíno.

Point your phone at the mural and watch as history comes alive. This web-based augmented reality experience layers 3D storytelling directly onto the artwork — no app download required.

Designed as part of a self-guided walking tour, the experience deepens the connection between Atlanta's vibrant street art scene and the rich cultural heritage of the Taíno people, the indigenous inhabitants of the Caribbean.

Powered by 8th Wall, the experience runs entirely in your mobile browser and is optimized for both iOS and Android.

The Initiative

Altera Media & the City of Atlanta

This AR experience is part of a broader collaboration between Altera Media, the City of Atlanta, and the 2026 FIFA World Cup — an initiative that uses augmented reality to celebrate Atlanta's public art and the diverse cultures represented in the city during one of the world's largest sporting events.

By anchoring digital storytelling to murals across the city, the project invites visitors and residents alike to engage with Atlanta's streets in a new way — discovering history, culture, and community through their phones.
